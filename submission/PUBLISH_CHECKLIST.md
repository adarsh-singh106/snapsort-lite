- [ ] Upload `submission/assets/cover.png` as the DEV cover image.
- [ ] Upload `submission/assets/before-after.png`, `architecture.png`, and the terminal evidence.

- [ ] Add a genuine demo video URL if one is recorded; otherwise leave the limitation explicit.
- [ ] Confirm the challenge window and whether late submissions are accepted.
- [ ] Confirm with DEV whether OCR-only Tesseract satisfies “open-source AI at its core.”
- [ ] Review the article for fictional-data labels and AI disclosure.
- [ ] Publish manually only after the above blockers are resolved.
